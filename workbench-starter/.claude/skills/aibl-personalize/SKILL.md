# Personalize

Get to know the person well enough to be useful, write it down where you will read it first every session, and let them correct you before it is saved.

## Inputs

Whatever is already in `context/`. Read it first. Never replace something they already told you with a default.

## Procedure

1. Ask only what is missing, one question at a time, in plain words, and wait for each answer. At most four questions: what they are trying to get done in the next month; who the result is for; which of their files or sources you may read (if `library/` is empty and they have nothing handy, offer to work from a short practice example instead, and say it is practice); and, only if useful, one question about how they like to work.

2. Write a short note from the answers: three to eight lines, plain sentences, no headings except a title. Show the whole note in the chat and ask them to correct anything you guessed. Say which lines were their words and which were your guesses.

3. Save only after they say it is right. Write it to `context/project.md`. If they mention something personal they would not want in a saved file, leave it out of the note and say why.

4. Say what you saved and where, in one line. Then offer one question they could ask you right now that only their note makes possible.

## Result and check

`context/project.md` exists, in their words, with at least one line they corrected or added. They can point to the guess you made and how they fixed it. You never saved without showing the note first.

## Recovery

If `context/project.md` already exists, read it back to them in three sentences and ask what changed, instead of starting over.
